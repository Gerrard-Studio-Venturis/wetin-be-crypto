## Recommendation and authority

Use **criterion-based checks and fictional missions**: meet every published required decision and its core rationale; designated essential decisions cannot be offset by unrelated answers. Begin with selected responses and structured rationale choices for automatic assessment. Free-text explanations can support facilitated research or separately staffed human review, but no automatic free-text grader is proposed.

The owner adopted the eight-module/32-lesson baseline and criterion-based structured assessment model under D-16/17. This is model approval, not qualified approval of exact criteria, complete banks, prerequisite versions, gates or examples. D-10 keeps public reading open. D-11's 30-day guest memory is a storage policy, not a mastery-expiry rule or learning-review interval. Fixtures are jurisdiction-neutral: a fictional payment/receipt setting does not establish legal availability or suitability in Ghana, Nigeria, or another country. No application code or visual design is produced.

For this proposal a complete module check contains evidence for all four module outcomes. A failed complete check can explain individually met criteria, but those findings do **not** automatically become reusable partial prerequisite credit. A narrower check/challenge must be explicitly approved as an evidence-producing activity, with its own published outcome set and criteria. This keeps a small sample from masquerading as a full module pass. Qualified reviewers may propose partial recognition later as a separate PD-02 rule.

## Grading, rationale, and independence

Define a criterion as a meaningful decision, interpretation, or explanation, not a keyword or an arbitrary point. Each activity version identifies its exact required criteria before starting. Show what is assessed and the passing rule without revealing the answers.

| Criterion class | Proposed treatment | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E — Essential decision | Every designated E criterion and its required rationale must be met. Any incorrect/omitted E criterion prevents passing the activity. | Refusing a recovery-material request; pausing an unsupported receiving route. |
| C — Required concept or application | Every mapped C criterion must be demonstrated for this criterion-based proposal. Give specific repair feedback when unmet. | Distinguishing the wallet interface from the ledger, or interpreting a supplied fee rule. |
| Optional extension | Feedback-only unless a future reviewed version explicitly adds it as required. | Naming a specialist consensus mechanism beyond the foundation task. |

There is **no universal percentage**. The proposed ordinary rule is all published C criteria met, alongside all E criteria. This is a product/assessment proposal, not a threshold derived from research. A future activity may use an approved ordinary score threshold, but must still identify required outcomes and preserve the essential-decision override. Do not impose spelling, Pidgin knowledge, confidence, speed, or prior crypto use as passing criteria.

For automatic assessment, a decision/rationale pair must both be correct where a rationale is required. Options must include a credible misconception and a useful pause/unknown action when the fixture warrants it. Do not make the correct response identifiable by length, repeated wording, or an always-correct network/brand. Where several answers are defensible, either accept each with its corresponding rationale or rewrite the question; do not guess the learner's intent. Missing required information should lead to a specific next check rather than inventing a fact.

English carries every required meaning. Equivalent clear wording, a harmless typo, or a different but valid route choice can pass facilitated review. Wrong amounts/units, altered recipient identities, or a missing essential condition change the meaning and need correction. Optional free text is not automatically graded; if human review is commissioned, show a pending-review state until that review is confirmed.

Provide a worked example before independent practice, then change the relevant decision conditions for the scored scenario. Learners may use public explanations; the claim is applied reasoning in an open-learning setting, not a proctored examination. Present feedback and answer explanations after submission. Learner-session extracts must omit the facilitator keys below and record any prior exposure. Eventual learner delivery must exclude unreleased keys and reviewer notes from public content, APIs, snippets, and caches.

## Retries, review, and versions

1. Record each submitted attempt separately. Confirm a pass only after authoritative grading or completed human review. Show unmet criteria, explanatory feedback, and useful lesson links.
2. Offer retry after feedback with a meaningful change to the decision conditions where an approved variant exists. Change support/recipient requirements, evidence stages, amounts/fee treatment, or claimed authority; shuffling choices alone is not a meaningful application variant. No punitive cooldown or fixed maximum is proposed.
3. If no reviewed variant exists, explain that the same scenario is repeated. Do not describe familiarity with its answer as stronger independent evidence. Missing/unapproved criteria or a known wrong key prevents offering the activity as current scored evidence.
4. Later failure preserves an earlier applicable pass and recommends targeted review. It does not automatically remove that pass or expire account evidence. A changed requirement, confirmed essential error, or guest expiry can separately affect current eligibility and must be explained.
5. Recommend review after a demonstrated gap or material relevant change, and offer voluntary future retrieval. No fixed weekly/monthly review schedule is established. Determine useful timing with learner evidence; S-08's general learning guidance does not prescribe a crypto interval.
6. Bind an attempt to its approved question/rubric/version at start. A normal replacement grades the attempt against its bound version; separately report current applicability. Outcome-neutral wording/voice edits do not invalidate achievements. A material criterion/outcome change requires an explicit equivalence/refresh decision.
7. On a confirmed essential content/key error, stop offering the affected version as current evidence and identify dependencies. Preserve in-flight responses with a paused/review-required state; explain and offer the corrected activity. Never confirm fresh current credit using the known wrong key. Retain original historical results/version plus correction/applicability notes; any qualified re-evaluation is a separate traceable review, not silent grading against newly substituted questions.

Retaining learning history does not override privacy retention or a guest's Clear memory action. Account/history retention and deletion policy remain separate PD-07/09 decisions. Guest evidence is authoritative only while available and eligible under the proposed technical mechanism; these Markdown examples award no stored pass.
