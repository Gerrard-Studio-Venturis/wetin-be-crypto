## How to read this register

S-01 to S-19 were carried forward from the earlier desk research. The revision date is not a new inspection date for every source. The [Research Findings](RESEARCH-FINDINGS.md) records actual 7 October 2026 inspections of S-02, S-08, and S-20 to S-24, including excerpt-only/provider/issuer limits. S-07 could not be reverified in this pass and is not used as the current sample's research basis. Subsequent research must add actual inspection dates, relevant extracts, product/version or country context, and links to the affected recommendation.

Distinguish:

- **Source observation:** what an inspected document or product surface supports.
- **Synthesis:** an interpretation assembled from evidence.
- **Approved policy:** an owner choice, not a claim that research proved it optimal.
- **Hypothesis:** an idea requiring investigation or learner review.
- **Unverified behaviour:** a proposed application outcome that has not been implemented/tested.

Public documentation can support a capability claim within its stated scope. It does not prove the proposed end-to-end application. Initial competitor research did not test logged-in journeys.

The preceding checkpoint recorded actual 7 October 2026 inspections S-25–46. This checkpoint adds S-47–50 in [Regional Scope Research](REGIONAL-SCOPE-RESEARCH.md), with historical and partial-PDF limits. Detailed methods/dates/limits remain in the linked source reports. Some URLs repeat baseline sources with a new explicit inspection record. No learner session or application test occurred. D-15 still excludes visual-design production while retaining accessibility and voice requirements.
