## Question and method

How should a West African focus affect the first examples and editorial work without implying that the same crypto route is appropriate or available everywhere?

Firecrawl searches found official Nigeria/Ghana material; some Nigeria-query results were from the US SEC and were excluded from Nigerian evidence. Four official surfaces were then retrieved with fresh-fetch requests on 7 October 2026. All final responses returned HTTP 200. Search excerpts, full HTML/PDF text and a capped PDF extract are distinguished below. No legal reconciliation, provider testing, current licence-directory check or learner session occurred.

## Regional scope recommendation

Use **Nigeria and Ghana as proposed initial example settings and research anchors**, with clearly fictional numerical inputs. This follows the approved West African focus, English-first starting scope and existing learner-review proposal; it is a planning judgement rather than evidence that these are the optimal markets. Keep the foundation readable by the wider audience. Include another West African context and a wider-African comparison in actual discovery when access permits; French publication remains a later decision.

Do not label the website as offering verified operational coverage for all countries. Before a country/provider-specific instruction is published, record the applicable claim, current official source, source/inspection dates, restrictions, reviewer and update trigger. If evidence is unresolved, use a jurisdiction-neutral educational fixture or hold the instruction. Do not quietly remove the country label while retaining facts that imply an unsupported local route.

The first asset/hub shortlist is separately proposed in [Launch Scope](LAUNCH-SCOPE.md). No live price, exchange, bank/mobile-money partnership, payout route, issuer redemption eligibility or tax treatment is selected or verified by this pass. [Editorial Operating Plan](EDITORIAL-OPERATING-PLAN.md) allocates source review; it does not claim staffing exists.

The source pass strengthens the freshness/applicability requirements while leaving PD-01 and human review open. No new owner approval is inferred from continuation.
