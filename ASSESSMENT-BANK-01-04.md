### Learner instructions shared by every form

All people, services, values, policies, addresses and identifiers in these forms are fictional classroom inputs. `DEMO-*` references are deliberately unusable destinations. Do not enter actual recovery material, open an external link, connect a wallet or move funds. Read the supplied records and choose **one action/finding A, B or C and one reason 1, 2 or 3 in every row**. Both selections must be correct. Review the stated required criteria before starting; all rows below are required, and no speed, spelling, confidence or Pidgin knowledge is assessed.

Rows labelled **E** propose an essential decision; a wrong essential answer cannot be offset by another answer. Rows labelled **C** are required concepts/applications. For the adopted criterion-based model, all required E and C pairs must be met to pass the named form. Essential classification and exact rubric still need qualified review. A failed module check may explain what was understood but supplies no automatic partial reusable gate credit. You may reread public learning material. Feedback follows submission; a retry changes the situation rather than just rearranging answers.
