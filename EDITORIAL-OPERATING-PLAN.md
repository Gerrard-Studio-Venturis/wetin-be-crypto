## Proposed seed manifest

Size launch preparation around **32 lessons across all eight modules**, their checks/missions and reviewed experienced-entry mappings, **six articles**, **six hubs**, and **24 glossary entries**. The smaller content-review pilot below is a sequencing option and does not remove foundation modules or change the learning promise. Seed articles are freely readable without lessons, enrolment, or accounts; reading/bookmarking earns no curriculum credit.

## Preparation workload and arithmetic

**Person-hours across functions**, not one person's calendar time or a price quote. Standard source checking, one ordinary revision cycle, copy editing, and item metadata are included. Estimates assume text-first English content, supplied fictional exercises, bounded selected-response activities, existing source leads, and no original field reporting. Difficult research gaps or changed rubrics can exceed the ranges.

| Experienced-entry mapping | Map reviewed variants to relevant outcomes, equivalence and applicability; **2–4h/module**. Assumes reuse, not a separate extensive challenge bank. | 8 × 2–4 = **16–32h**. |
| Glossary | Definition/example, source check, subject/English review, metadata: **1–2h**. | 24 × 1–2 = **24–48h**. |
| Hub | Reviewed introduction, identities/aliases, limits, sources and links: **3–5h**. | 6 × 3–5 = **18–30h**. |
| Explainer | Research 1–2h; draft 1.5–2.5h; subject review 0.75–1.25h; edit 0.5–0.75h; ordinary rework/metadata 0.25–0.5h: **4–7h**. | 4 × 4–7 = **16–28h**. |

Foundation lessons plus checks/missions/challenge mapping: **240–448 + 64–112 + 48–80 + 16–32 = 368–672h**. Reference seed: **24–48 + 18–30 = 42–78h**. Article seed: **16–28 + 8–14 = 24–42h**. Shared audit/human review: **18–30 + 22–38 + 8–16 = 48–84h**.

Base preparation: **368–672 + 42–78 + 24–42 + 48–84 = 482–876h**. Add a proposed **20% uncertainty reserve**, 96.4–175.2h, for extra review/rework: **578.4–1,051.2h**, rounded upwards to a planning allowance of **580–1,055 person-hours**. This reserve is judgement, not an evidence-based contingency rate. It does not guarantee coverage of a major scope change.

## Sustainable weekly model and pilot choices

**Preferred later editorial pilot:** one original evergreen article each week, plus **at most one qualifying sourced summary per fortnight**. Stories can replace an evergreen slot with their extra effort budgeted. This is a capacity proposal for a future approved operation, not a publication promise. It keeps room for foundation/reference upkeep and corrections while the editorial process is measured.

| Weekly work | Pilot allowance | Faster two-item model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New article production including normal review | Explainer 4–7h + averaged half-summary 1–2h = **5–9h**. | Explainer 4–7h + summary 2–4h = **6–11h**. |
| Bounded source/risk monitoring | **2–3h**. | **2–3h**. |
| Foundation/reference maintenance | **2–3h**. | **2–3h**. |
| Fluent voice batch checks | **1–2h**. | **1–2h**. |
| Triage, scheduling and coordination | **1–2h**. | **1–2h**. |
| Reserved correction/rework capacity | **3–5h**. | **3–5h**. |
| Total person-hours | **14–24h/week**. | **15–26h/week**. |

The half-summary is an accounting average; use a fortnightly **28–48h** pilot allocation to absorb uneven production. Unused correction time can support reviewed backlog work, but must remain available when needed. A Story's 6–10h replaces, rather than quietly joins, the 4–7h explainer slot. Delayed review, no suitable news, or urgent corrections means fewer publications.

Indicative allocation of the **same** pilot pool: writer/producer 8–13h; subject/source reviewer 3–5h; fluent voice reviewer 1–2h; publishing editor 2–4h = **14–24h**. Functions can overlap where the owner approves, but this is not a second budget or evidence that people are available. Named backup availability and specialist escalations must be agreed.

New foundation production is additional: one lesson plus its share of module assessment work requires **7.5–14 + (16–28)/4 = 11.5–21h**. Two new lessons per week therefore add **23–42h**, taking a simultaneous pilot/article-and-foundation operation to **37–66h/week**, before one-time reference setup/recruitment. Arithmetic is workload, not a delivery schedule; batching and reviewer waiting time can interrupt output.

First review the representative M-02/M-04/M-07 samples under Launch Scope. Then rehearse a complete-module content pilot before wider production: complete the four M-04 lessons, the full proposed KC-04/PM-04 outcome coverage, one explainer, and related reference material. Existing KC-04/PM-04 samples demonstrate **O-04.2 only**; they do not satisfy the full proposed gate or complete M-04. Record actual time by task and critical findings, then revise estimates. Review six editorial cycles before increasing cadence; this interval is a proposal, not a demonstrated ramp rule. Preserve all eight foundation modules in the preparation manifest unless the owner explicitly changes scope.

| Function | Responsibility | Backup/authority condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product owner | Approve scope, countries/models, cadence/budget and review independence; appoint people and access. | Name a delegated decision maker for absence; no delegation exists merely because this plan proposes one. |
| Writer / editorial producer | Original draft, claim-source packet, fictional/actual labels, relationships, versions and revision actions. | Another briefed writer can continue the packet; cannot inherit an approval automatically. |
| Qualified crypto/source reviewer | Check relevant mechanics, jurisdiction/provider/issuer scope, essential criteria, variants and dependencies. | Named suitably qualified backup; otherwise hold affected substantive publication. |
| English/fluent voice reviewer | Essential English, natural varied Pidgin, serious tone, repetition and reviewed context. | Qualified replacement or defer/remove the optional aside through a reviewed edit; no mechanical phrase rotation. |
| Publishing editor | Approve the exact revision, sources, dates/labels and public relationships; own corrections queue. | Named editor with emergency authority, audit responsibility and subsequent-review route. |
| Research facilitator / note-taker | Consent, neutral tasks, controlled rubric exposure, anonymised observations and synthesis. | Owner-arranged coverage; participants do not act as source authorities. |
| Later technical operator | Publish/hold permissions, private keys/records, approved-revision search/cache updates and recoverable operations. | Delivery ownership required later; current documentation proves no working controls. |

Propose distinct human subject-review and publishing-approval identities for substantive safety material, with writer self-approval prevented. Owner review must settle independence/overlap and the capability policy. [Component research](WORDPRESS-SUPPORTING-COMPONENTS.md) documents default-role publishing exceptions; a revision queue alone does not enforce the proposed review chain.

Follow FS-11/FL-10: draft/source packet → named-revision subject review → English/voice checks → publishing-editor approval → actual publication record. Material changes invalidate relevant approval, including substantive metadata/asset relationships. Unapproved ordinary updates retain the approved public revision. Missing source/reviewer capacity is a hold, not elapsed-time approval.

When a credible error is identified, record affected identity/version, claim, evidence, dependencies and triage owner. Known essential teaching/assessment errors require the authorised pause/correction path before current evidence is offered. Preserve original attempts; determine current refresh/applicability explicitly. Emergency edits require editor authority, reason/audit, and subsequent subject review. Visible material corrections explain what changed/when; spelling edits do not reset publication/verification dates. Later French dependencies are flagged when translations exist. Reader-facing correction functions and technical behaviour follow the existing specification; no new public reporting feature is added here.

## Review frequency and quality constraints

Proposed schedules are staffing policies, not source-proven optimal intervals. Maintain a bounded monitored-source list and named item owner, not a promise to monitor every asset or country.

| Content risk | Proposed review trigger |
| --- | --- |
| Core definitions/conceptual introductions | Before publication; on relevant reported/source changes; scheduled six-month review. |
| Custody, authorisation, compatibility and status explanations | Before publication; immediate triage of a relevant confirmed change; quarterly queued review. |
| Issuer/provider/country-specific claims | Recheck before publication/material update and on source changes. Keep named route instructions out unless current applicability is established. |
| News | Verify event/source/publication dates before release; correct material errors. Preserve archive context instead of making old reporting look new. |
| Check/mission versions | Before being offered; whenever supporting teaching/criteria change or an essential error is confirmed. Review historical versus current applicability. |

An overdue review does not automatically make a stable definition false. If essential freshness cannot be established, hold the affected practical instruction/activity rather than imply current verification. Maintenance estimates cover routine checks, not mass protocol changes or an incident response surge. Corrections take priority over output targets.

Initial coverage must distinguish a fictional country setting from legal/provider applicability. Nigerian or Ghanaian names/currencies do not establish that a crypto wage, invoice, deposit or payout route is permitted or available. Country-specific claims need current applicable sources and reviewer scope; a literacy announcement or historical regulation is insufficient. French readiness supplies identities/dependencies, not French content or validated Francophone comprehension.

Publication quality requires source/claim traceability, correct identities, meaningful dates, independent usefulness, approved mapped criteria, and all required meaning in English. Pidgin has no per-item quota. Review actual batches for repetition of structure as well as words, with restrained English for fraud, losses, serious instructions and corrections. Text/media alternatives and accessible labels are content duties; working accessibility remains a later delivery verification.

Countries, sample mix, compensation and access remain unconfirmed; overlapping tasks/roles do not create extra participants.

The owner must arrange volunteers through appropriate channels, appoint a facilitator/note-taker, agree eligibility and fixed incentives if any, and provide language/device/access support. A Francophone conversation needs suitable facilitation; interpreter-assisted answers must not be reported as independent English comprehension. Record missed geographic/language/access coverage rather than claim region-wide validation.

Before sessions, approve privacy/withdrawal/retention arrangements, separate contact details from repository notes, and consent for notes/optional recordings/quotation use. Participants need no funds, wallet access, credentials or account registration. Prepare exact sample versions and private rubric separation; use T-01–T-05 for teaching/article comprehension and the remaining guide tasks for progress, optional memory, import and saving interpretations as time permits. Record initial responses before help, exposure/order, assistance and actual observations separately from inference.

Fluent review assesses naturalness of actual wording; non-Nigerian participants paraphrase meaning and assess whether English stands alone. Learners' preferences do not establish crypto accuracy. Review of Markdown/written states cannot prove website discoverability, grading, saving, keyboard operation or connectivity performance. Critical comprehension findings need source/rubric review and focused repeat tasks; further rounds beyond the allowance need additional access/time. If access is unavailable, retain the protocol and report direct learner/voice validation as not conducted.
